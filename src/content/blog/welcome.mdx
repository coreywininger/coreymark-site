# Welcome

This site is a home for the writing I do about IT — and a few weekend
projects that don't fit anywhere else. It's a placeholder right now while
I wire everything up, but the plan is simple: publish things that would
be useful to the person I was five years ago, and avoid pretending I know
more than I do.

## Three audiences, one roof

The writing sits in four deliberate lanes:

- **IT operations** — the discipline of running infrastructure
- **Tech ↔ business** — translating engineering reality to leadership
- **AI for IT** — what it actually does, not what the vendors say
- **Hybrid cloud** — workload placement, cost, honest tradeoffs

Plus a personal lane for everything else.

## What to expect

Short posts when an idea is short. Long posts when it isn't. No hot
takes, no jargon, no fabricated metrics. If I don't know something, I'll
say so.

More soon.
