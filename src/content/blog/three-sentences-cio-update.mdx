## The template

Every exec update I've written that actually worked boiled down to three
sentences:

1. **What changed this period that the business cares about.** One
   sentence. Business outcomes, not tickets closed.
2. **What's at risk and what I'm doing about it.** One sentence. Risk
   without a response plan is just noise.
3. **What I need from you.** One sentence. If nothing, say "nothing" — it
   builds credibility for when you do need something.

## Why it works

Executives read hundreds of updates a week. The three-sentence format
respects their time and forces the IT leader to actually decide what
matters. A 14-bullet status dump is avoidance dressed as thoroughness.
